About this demo: definitions and sample data
Sample data only
Every number on this page is synthetic, generated for demonstration. The national totals are set near the scale of a high-burden country in recent World Malaria Reports but are not WHO figures. District-level data exist for 9 states; the EMR exchange is treated as live in 10 states.
WHO estimate
Modelled total malaria cases or deaths, including those that never reach a health service.
Expected per reporting system
The estimate split by where people are expected to seek care (public facility, private facility, EMR-connected facility, community health worker) or to get no formal care. In practice this split would come from care-seeking surveys (DHS/MIS), facility listings and system coverage.
No formal care
Cases and deaths not expected to reach any reporting system (no care sought, informal drug sellers, home deaths). They are a modelling gap rather than a reporting gap, so they are excluded from the gap ranking.
% captured
Reported ÷ expected for that system and location.
Reporting completeness and recording accuracy
Completeness is the share of units (facilities or CHWs) expected to report that did. Recording accuracy is reported ÷ (expected × completeness): how fully units that do report record the cases or deaths they see.
Suggested focus
A simple rule: EMR exchange not live → roll it out; completeness under 70% → raise reporting completeness; recording accuracy under 70% → improve recording and data quality; otherwise maintain and verify through data quality audits.
